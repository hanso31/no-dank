## How it works
Activated carbon has a vast internal surface area — a single gram has roughly
the surface of a tennis court. Odor molecules adhere to this surface (adsorption)
and stay there. Nothing is masked, nothing is covered up: the odor disappears
because it is bound.

**The difference from air fresheners:** sprays add a new smell.
Activated carbon removes the old one.

## Dosing
Rule of thumb: **50–100 g of activated carbon per m³ of room volume.**
One pouch (50 g) is ideal for small, enclosed spaces up to approx. 1 m³ —
e.g. cabinets, bin areas, bathrooms, grow tents up to 60×60 cm.

**For larger spaces:** distribute several pouches (multi-pouch strategy) instead
of buying one large product. This keeps contact surface per volume high.

## Use with a fan
In active spaces (e.g. a grow tent with air circulation), the pouch works best
in the airflow: hang it in the path of the fan. Continuous air movement keeps
bringing new odor molecules to the carbon — performance increases significantly
compared to still air.

## Care & replacement
- Store the pouch airtight until use; **open only before use**
- Saturation: after weeks to months (depending on odor load) the surface is full
- Regeneration: heat the pouch at 120 °C for 2–3 hours in an oven → works again
  (limited if a grease/moisture layer has built up — replace)
- In practice: check every 4–8 weeks under heavy load
